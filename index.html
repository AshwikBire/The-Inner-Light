ABOUT THE BOOK

A companion for the parts of life
that don't come with instructions.

Most of us reach a point where the old answers stop working — where achievement, routine, or willpower alone can no longer explain the quiet weight we're carrying. The Inner Light was written for that point.

Across an introduction, fifteen chapters, and a closing reflection, the book moves between two traditions that are rarely given equal weight: positive psychology's evidence-based understanding of what helps people thrive, and spirituality's older, slower vocabulary for meaning, pain, and awareness. Neither is asked to replace the other.

It is not a book of formulas. It is an invitation to sit with the questions life keeps asking — about identity, pain, purpose, forgiveness, and gratitude — and to leave with a little more space between yourself and your thoughts.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Ashwik Bire

Data Scientist · Researcher · Author — Pune, Maharashtra, India

Ashwik built his career at the intersection of data science, analytics, and technology. In May 2025, at twenty-four, a posterior circulation stroke added an unexpected chapter to that story — nine days in the ICU at Ruby Hall Clinic, Hinjawadi, Pune, followed by a recovery that combined medical treatment with scientific self-study, psychology research, and intentional healing practice.

He studied the neuroscience of stroke recovery, applied principles of positive psychology and neuroplasticity to his own rehabilitation, and documented the process with the same precision he brings to his professional work. The Inner Light carries that same instinct forward: a belief that lived experience and scientific understanding are strongest when held together.
